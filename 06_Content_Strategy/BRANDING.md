# REDIEU Z Branding

## Brand
REDIEU Z

## Watermark
`@REDIEUZ`

## Placement
Keep the watermark away from the top edge and likely cropping zones.

Position it consistently in a safe lower region, with sufficient spacing from platform interface overlays.

## Visual consistency
- One clear brand identity.
- Avoid duplicated logos.
- Avoid incorrect or improvised logos.
- Keep watermark scale consistent.
- Ensure readability on bright and dark footage.
- Use consistent typography and caption styling.

## Video identity
The content should be recognisable as REDIEU Z without obstructing the gameplay.
