# REDIEU Z Content Strategy

## Research targets

Find high-performing GTA 6 content across:
- Gameplay.
- Trailers.
- Official announcements.
- News.
- Analysis.
- Character discussions.
- Map discoveries.
- Mechanics.
- Community theories.

## Research metadata

For each source, collect when available:
- Title.
- Channel.
- URL.
- View count.
- Duration.
- Publication date.
- Views per day.
- Transcript availability.
- Main topic.
- Source credibility.

## Topic scoring

Consider:
1. Audience interest.
2. Current relevance.
3. Source reliability.
4. Visual availability.
5. Potential for a strong hook.
6. Originality.
7. Suitability for a short video.

## Script requirements

Every Short should contain:
- Strong opening hook.
- One central subject.
- Clear narrative.
- Relevant supporting facts.
- Natural English narration.
- Synchronised captions.
- Closing payoff or question.
- Title.
- Description.
- Hashtags.
- Source references.
- Fact/rumour classification.

## Fact classification

CONFIRMED:
Supported by official Rockstar material or reliable primary evidence.

UNCONFIRMED:
Rumours, leaks, theories or claims not officially established.

Do not present speculation as confirmed gameplay.

## Footage requirements

Prioritise:
- Original gameplay recordings.
- Footage created specifically for REDIEU Z.
- Explicitly licensed media.
- Official assets only where their terms permit the intended use.

A publicly accessible YouTube video is not automatically free to download, edit or republish.

## Scene-to-narration mapping

Every scene must have a reason to exist.

Example:
Narration about vehicle customisation must be accompanied by relevant vehicle customisation footage, not unrelated driving footage.

## Audio

- Narration must remain clear.
- Background music should support the mood.
- Duck music beneath speech.
- Avoid clipping.
- Use properly licensed music.
