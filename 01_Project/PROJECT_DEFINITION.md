# Project Definition

## Brand
REDIEU Z

## Initial niche
Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6).

## Primary objective
Create a repeatable content operation capable of producing multiple high-quality original Shorts and longer videos.

## Target platforms
- YouTube Shorts and YouTube videos.
- TikTok.
- Instagram Reels.
- Facebook Reels.

## Content categories
1. Official GTA 6 announcements.
2. Confirmed gameplay information.
3. Trailer analysis.
4. Character and story analysis.
5. Map and location analysis.
6. Gameplay mechanics.
7. News and developments.
8. Rumour analysis, explicitly labelled.
9. Comparisons and theories.
10. Interesting community discoveries.

## Editorial language
English is the default language for published scripts, narration, captions, descriptions and hashtags.

## Visual identity
- Brand: REDIEU Z.
- Watermark should use `@REDIEUZ`.
- Watermark must be positioned away from the top edge and other vulnerable crop areas.
- Keep the watermark visible but unobtrusive.
- Use consistent typography, colour and branding.
- Avoid duplicated logos or incorrect branding.

## Video format
Primary format: vertical 9:16, 1080 x 1920, 30 FPS.

Target duration for initial Shorts: approximately 25–45 seconds.

## Quality requirement
Every visual sequence must be relevant to the narration. Random trailer footage must not be used simply to fill the timeline.
