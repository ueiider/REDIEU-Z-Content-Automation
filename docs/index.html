REDIEUZ Content Automation

A local-first workflow for researching, scripting, rendering, reviewing and preparing original REDIEUZ video content.

What it does

REDIEUZ Content Automation supports the production of original short-form and longer video content. It can research candidate topics, maintain scripts, generate narration and captions, render vertical videos, run quality checks, and organize finished files for human review.

Human approval before publication

Rendered content is prepared for review. Publication is not automatic from rendering. A human operator approves content and controls when and where it is published.

Open-Dispatch integration

The project uses Open-Dispatch as an internal publishing queue and dashboard for approved content. Open-Dispatch can queue publication jobs for supported platforms and track their status. Platform credentials are configured separately by the operator.

YouTube authorization

YouTube publishing uses Google OAuth for a desktop application. The authorized Google account grants permission to upload videos to the connected REDIEUZ YouTube channel. Credentials and tokens are stored in local application/profile storage and are not published in this repository.

Platform support

YouTube authorization is the primary configured platform. Other platforms shown by the publishing queue, such as Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, require their own credentials, review or approval steps, and may require publicly accessible video URLs depending on the platform API.

Local-first data handling

Production files, scripts, logs, queues and review packages are kept on the operator's local machine. Secrets, OAuth files, tokens and environment configuration are excluded from version control.

This website is separate from the private publishing dashboard. It does not provide access to credentials, internal endpoints, queue controls or configuration.
