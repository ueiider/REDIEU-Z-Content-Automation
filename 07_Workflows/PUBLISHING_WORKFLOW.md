# Publishing Workflow

## Objective
Automate approved content distribution to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

## Before dispatch
- Video approved.
- Correct platform format.
- Correct caption and title.
- Description and hashtags verified.
- Source and footage rights checked.
- Correct publishing account selected.
- Visibility checked.

## Queue states
Recommended persistent states:

DISCOVERED
RESEARCHED
SCRIPTED
FACT_CHECKED
ASSETS_READY
RENDERING
READY_FOR_REVIEW
APPROVED
QUEUED_FOR_PUBLISHING
PUBLISHING
PUBLISHED
FAILED
CANCELLED

## Reliability
- Do not duplicate uploads after network errors.
- Store platform publication IDs.
- Retry only when safe.
- Preserve failed items for investigation.
- Keep a publication log.

## Approval
No public upload without explicit approval during the initial implementation.
