# Production Workflow

## Stage 1 — Discover
Search current GTA 6 topics, gameplay, official announcements and trending videos.

## Stage 2 — Research
Collect metadata, source links and available transcripts.

## Stage 3 — Analyse
Use Nemotron through the local AI workflow to identify strong original topics.

## Stage 4 — Write
Generate original scripts in English.

## Stage 5 — Verify
Check factual statements and label speculation.

## Stage 6 — Plan visuals
Map each narration segment to a matching scene.

## Stage 7 — Produce narration
Use the configured TTS engine.

## Stage 8 — Edit
Add gameplay, captions, music and watermark.

## Stage 9 — Render
Generate vertical 1080x1920 output.

## Stage 10 — Quality control
Check:
- Correct resolution.
- Audio.
- Caption timing.
- Visual relevance.
- Branding.
- Copyright and licence.
- Duration.
- Metadata.

## Stage 11 — Review
Save finished videos in:

`D:\GTA6_Content\03_Shorts\Ready_For_Review`

## Stage 12 — Approve
Require explicit approval before publishing.

## Stage 13 — Dispatch
Use Open-Dispatch to send approved content to configured platforms.

## Stage 14 — Record
Save platform response, publication ID, URL, date and status.
